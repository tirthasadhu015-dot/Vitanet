About Vitanet

How Vitanet works

Vitanet connects donors, patients, and verified hospitals through transparent matching and accountable settlement.

Donors and patients register their compatible organ information. Hospitals complete their profile and AI-assisted Google Maps verification before searching for an organ.

The platform then records compatible matches, protects the escrow workflow, and gives hospitals a clear dashboard for verification.
